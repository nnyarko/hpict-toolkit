# Changelog

All notable changes to HPI-CT are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-08 (first open release)
- **Fully open licensing:** MIT (code) + CC BY 4.0 (content & data). Free to use, adapt, and deploy with attribution.
- **Redesigned toolkit UI:** ten flat tabs collapse into three modes — *Assess*, *Look up*, *Why*; a 15-question triage produces a real provisional result (maturity, weakest areas, and the controls that close them); search replaces browsing the full control list.
- **160 controls** across 9 modules (8 control-bearing + governance); **224-question** evidence-anchored assessment; rules dictionary, data-source registry, and control-failure diagnosis matrix.
- **De-identified case exemplars** in `/data` distilled from over 1,550 coded DOJ enforcement actions (identified dataset held by the maintainer).
- Machine-readable exports (JSON + CSV) for controls, rules, data sources, diagnosis matrix, questionnaire, and case exemplars.

_The row-level identified case dataset and raw intake are not part of this release; they remain with the maintainer and are available to researchers on request._
