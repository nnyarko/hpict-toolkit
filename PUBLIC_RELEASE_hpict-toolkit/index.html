HPI-CT — Healthcare Program Integrity Control Toolkit

A free, open toolkit that helps healthcare payers, providers, and program-integrity agencies find and close the internal-control gaps that fraud, waste, and abuse exploit — before payment. Aligned to the GAO Green Book (2025) and COSO.

What you can do

  • Assess — a 15-question triage gives a provisional maturity read, your weakest areas, and the controls that close them; go deeper when ready.
  • Look up — search the 160-control library instead of scrolling it.
  • Why — see the evidence and the regulation behind each control.

Runs entirely in your browser, offline — no server, no account, nothing to install. New here? Start with the Quick-Start and User Guide.
