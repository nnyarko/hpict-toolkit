# Contributing to HPI-CT

Thank you for helping strengthen the fight against healthcare fraud, waste, and abuse. HPI-CT is a free, open, public-source project created and maintained by **Nicholas Nyarko**. Contributions are welcome and curated.

## Ways to contribute

- **Propose or refine a control** — include the fraud scheme / failure mode it addresses, the operational stage, a concrete trigger, owner, evidence artifact, failure mode, and a primary-source regulatory citation (eCFR / CMS / OIG / GAO).
- **Report a citation or data error** — open an Issue with the control ID (e.g., `PE-04`) and the corrected source.
- **Suggest a case correction** — all case references are allegations; if a matter was acquitted, dismissed, or vacated, tell us with a source and we'll correct or remove it.
- **Add a mapping** — a commercial-side regulatory hook, a REG-ID / DS-ID link, or an analytics technique.
- **Share how you used it** — open a Discussion or send a testimonial; this helps the project and other adopters.

## How to submit

Open a GitHub **Issue** (or **Discussion** for open-ended ideas); for concrete edits, submit a **Pull Request** referencing it. Keep changes scoped to one control, citation, or mapping where possible. A maintainer reviews, verifies against primary sources, and merges.

## Terms (please read)

- Contributions are accepted under the project's open licenses — **CC BY 4.0** (content/data) and **MIT** (code) — with **Nicholas Nyarko as maintainer and steward** of the canonical version.
- You confirm you have the right to contribute the material and that it contains **no PHI, claim-level data, beneficiary/provider identifiers, or other non-public data** — public sources only.
- Contributors are credited in `CHANGELOG.md`. **Credit is not ownership:** contributing does not transfer ownership of the framework, its name, or the maintainer-held identified dataset.

## Scope & safety

The toolkit is **advisory** — it recommends controls; it does not block or decide any payment, coverage, or eligibility action. Keep proposals within that advisory scope.
